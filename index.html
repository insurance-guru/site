Super

Why default cover is rarely enough

Read more

Default cover is automatic and easy to get, but it's designed to keep premiums low, not coverage strong. Amounts are often too small for a mortgage, definitions are tighter, and cover can stop at certain ages or when your balance runs low.

Premiums

Variable (level) or variable age (stepped): which is the better structure?

Read more

Variable age (stepped) premiums start lower and rise as you get older. Variable (level) premiums start higher and stay steadier as you age, though insurers can still review rates. The right choice depends on how long you plan to hold the cover and what you can sustain in retirement.

TPD

TPD: own occupation or any occupation?

Read more

Own occupation TPD can pay if you cannot return to your own job. Any occupation TPD pays only if you cannot do any work you are suited to by education, training or experience. The definition can change the outcome of a claim.

Income

What is income protection?

Read more

Income protection replaces part of your income if illness or injury stops you working. It is paid regularly after a waiting period, for up to a set benefit period. Those definitions and periods all affect what you receive.

Health

Can I get cover with a pre-existing condition?

Read more

Many people with a pre-existing condition can still get cover. Insurers assess each case on its own, and terms can be adjusted, for example with a loading or an exclusion. Assessing health up front gives more certainty about what will be paid.
